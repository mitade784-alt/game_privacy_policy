Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Rishi Foundation ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the TRIJON mobile application.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers.

  • Avatar Photos: We use Android's zero-permission Photo Picker. Selected photos are saved strictly locally on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers.
  • Game Data: All in-game statistics, themes, and settings are saved locally on your device using Jetpack DataStore.

3. Third-Party Services

TRIJON operates entirely standalone and does not integrate third-party analytics trackers, data brokers, or advertising SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our Service does not transmit data to external servers, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13.
